🌱 ABOUT BUTTAR IMPEX

Rooted in Integrity, Growing for Global Markets

Founded in 2022, Buttar Impex has rapidly established itself as a premier agricultural commodity trading, importing, and exporting powerhouse. Headquartered in Gujranwala, Pakistan, we specialize in sourcing and supplying the finest agricultural produce to discerning international and domestic clients.

We are deeply committed to sustainable agricultural practices, partnering directly with experienced farmers to maintain exceptional purity, nutritional integrity, and freshness from farm gate to destination port.
